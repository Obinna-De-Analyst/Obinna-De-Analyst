## Data Analyst | Data Scientist

I believe the most valuable part of data isn't the numbers themselves—it's the story they reveal and the decisions they help us make.

My work begins with questions. Why is this happening? What are we missing? What patterns are hidden beneath the surface? I use analytics, visualization, statistical methods, and machine learning to investigate those questions and translate complex datasets into findings people can actually understand and use.

For me, a successful analysis doesn't end with a model or dashboard. It ends when the analysis provides a clearer answer to a real problem.
